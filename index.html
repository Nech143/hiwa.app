Find YouTube niches where small channels get big views

hiwa.app is a desktop application for content creators. You enter a topic, and hiwa.app shows public YouTube videos that perform far above their channel's size.

Contact us

What hiwa.app does

Keyword search

Search public YouTube videos by topic, language, upload date and duration.

×N ratio

Shows how many times a video's views exceed its channel's subscriber count.

Niche modes

Ready-made filters: anomalies, growing channels and new channels.

Videos and Shorts

Analyze regular videos and Shorts separately.

History and export

Recent searches are kept on your computer for 30 days. Results can be saved as a CSV file.

How hiwa.app uses YouTube data

  • hiwa.app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to read public information about videos and channels: titles, thumbnails, view counts, subscriber counts and publish dates.
  • hiwa.app does not ask you to sign in with Google and has no access to your YouTube or Google account.
  • hiwa.app runs on your own computer. Your searches and results are stored only there and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • The developer has no servers that receive your data. hiwa.app has no ads and no analytics.
